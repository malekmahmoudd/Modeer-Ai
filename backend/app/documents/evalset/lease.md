# Residential Lease Agreement

This lease is between Nile Homes Property Management (the landlord) and the tenant named on the signature page, for the furnished two-bedroom flat at 14 Garden Street, apartment 5.

# Term and rent

The lease runs for twelve months starting on the first of October. Monthly rent is 18,000 payable by bank transfer on or before the fifth day of each month. A late fee of 500 applies if rent arrives after the tenth. Rent will not increase during the first twelve months.

# Security deposit

The tenant pays a security deposit equal to two months' rent before moving in. The deposit is returned within thirty days of the end of the lease, minus the cost of any damage beyond normal wear and tear, together with an itemised list of deductions.

# Ending the lease early

Either party may end the lease early by giving sixty days' written notice. If the tenant leaves before the end of the first six months, they forfeit one month of the deposit. Notice must be sent by email to the property manager and is effective from the day it is received.

# Pets and guests

Small pets such as cats and small dogs are allowed with the landlord's written approval and an additional pet deposit of 3,000. Guests may stay for up to fourteen consecutive nights; longer stays require the landlord's consent. Smoking is not allowed anywhere inside the flat or on the balcony.

# Repairs and maintenance

The landlord is responsible for structural repairs, plumbing, electrical faults and the air conditioning units. The tenant must report problems within three days and is responsible for replacing light bulbs and for damage they cause. Emergency repairs, such as a burst pipe, can be reported by phone at any time.

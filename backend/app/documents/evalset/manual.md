# BaristaPro 300 Espresso Machine — User Manual

Thank you for choosing the BaristaPro 300. Read this manual before first use and keep it for reference. The machine is for household use only and must be plugged into an earthed socket.

# First use

Rinse the water tank and fill it with fresh cold water up to the MAX line. Switch the machine on and wait for the ready light to stop flashing, which takes about forty seconds. Run two cups of hot water through the group head without coffee to flush the system before making your first espresso.

# Making espresso

Use 18 grams of finely ground coffee for a double shot. Tamp evenly with about 15 kilograms of pressure, lock the portafilter into the group head and press the double-cup button. A good extraction takes 25 to 30 seconds. If the coffee runs too fast, grind finer; if it drips slowly, grind coarser.

# Descaling

Descale every two months, or when the descale light turns orange. Mix 500 millilitres of water with one sachet of BaristaPro descaler in the tank, place a large container under the group head and steam wand, and hold the steam and hot-water buttons together for five seconds to start the cycle. The cycle takes about twenty minutes. Rinse the tank and run a full tank of clean water afterwards.

# Error codes

E1 means the water tank is empty or not seated properly. E2 means the machine is overheating; switch it off for thirty minutes. E3 indicates a blocked group head or a grind that is too fine. E4 is a sensor fault that requires service. If an error persists after restarting, contact customer support.

# Warranty

The machine is covered by a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage from limescale if the machine was not descaled as instructed, nor damage from using the machine commercially. Keep your receipt as proof of purchase.
